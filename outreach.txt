Outreach Audience Framework
Your agents should target people most likely to:
make decisions
influence growth
approve collaborations
manage community operations
respond quickly
Do not target random members.


Outreach Priority Order
Tier 1 — Core Decision Makers
Highest priority targets.
Founders / Core Team
Best overall targets.
Common indicators:
founder
co-founder
ceo
core contributor
builder
team lead
Signals:
admin status
project announcements
speaks on behalf of project
linked X account
hosts spaces/interviews
high reply visibility
Best use case:
strategic partnerships
growth systems
community expansion
long-term collaboration


Growth / Marketing Leads
Usually easier to reach than founders and highly conversion-focused.
Titles:
head of growth
marketing lead
growth lead
partnerships
bizdev
ecosystem lead
community growth
Best use case:
campaigns
engagement systems
KOL outreach
user acquisition
community activation


Community Managers / Lead Moderators
Highest-response audience in Telegram-first workflows.
Signals:
daily activity
moderates discussions
answers members
welcomes users
coordinates announcements
Titles:
community manager
moderator
admin
ambassador lead
Best use case:
community engagement
group activity
growth coordination
warm introductions to founders


Tier 2 — Secondary Targets
Partnerships / Ecosystem Team
Good for collaborations and exposure-related outreach.
Titles:
partnerships
ecosystem
business development
collabs
integration lead


Influencer / KOL Managers
Useful for projects already running influencer campaigns.
Signals:
interacts with creators
coordinates promotions
manages KOL campaigns
active on X


Tier 3 — Strategic Community Members
Use when direct team access is difficult.
Ideal traits:
high engagement
trusted by admins
large X following
frequent replies
active voice in community
Purpose:
warm introductions
relationship mapping
internal referrals
Exclude These Profiles
Do not prioritize:
inactive admins
spam accounts
support bots
airdrop hunters
silent members
unverified accounts

Profile Filter Logic
Your profile-filter.ts should score users dynamically.
Example structure:
{
  "username": "nova_growth",
  "role": "growth_lead",
  "priority": "high",
  "score": 92,
  "signals": [
    "admin",
    "high activity",
    "linked X",
    "team interactions",
    "community authority"
  ]
}

Scoring Model
High Priority Signals
admin badge
founder keywords
growth-related keywords
linked socials
high message frequency
engagement from members
visible authority


Medium Priority Signals
moderator role
ecosystem role
partnership-related activity
active contributor


Low Priority Signals
low activity
no socials
minimal interaction
generic usernames


Telegram Workflow Logic
Telegram should always be prioritized over X.
Workflow:
IF telegram exists:
    join group
    analyze members
    identify team
    rank targets
    generate outreach drafts
Priority order inside Telegram:
1. Founder
2. Growth lead
3. Partnerships lead
4. Community manager
5. Moderator
6. Connected community member
Reason:
community managers reply fastest
founders make final decisions
growth leads convert best


X Fallback Workflow
Use only when Telegram is unavailable.
Workflow:
IF telegram == null AND x != null:
    analyze X account
    identify founder/team
    generate personalized outreach
    notify HITL
Priority targets:
founders
co-founders
growth leads
partnership managers
Avoid generic support or announcement accounts.


Adaptive Outreach Logic
Your outreach agent should adapt messaging by role.
Example:
IF role == founder:
    focus on scale and growth

IF role == growth_lead:
    focus on acquisition and engagement

IF role == community_manager:
    focus on activity and community health

IF role == partnerships:
    focus on collaboration opportunities
